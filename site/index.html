🔍 NH Real Estate Septic Evaluations vs. MA Title 5 Inspections

Selling or buying a house near the NH/MA border? Here is how septic inspections differ between Rockingham County, NH and Essex County, MA:

  • New Hampshire (Danville, Derry, Salem, Hampstead, Exeter): Standard home purchase & sale agreements include a comprehensive NH Septic Evaluation ($375–$550). The inspector locates the tank, checks scum/sludge depth, inspects inlet/outlet baffles and effluent filters, runs a hydraulic flow test, and probes the leach field. (Plus: under NH RSA 485-A:39, waterfront properties must obtain a formal NHDES Site Assessment prior to sale).
  • Massachusetts (Haverhill, Amesbury, Methuen, Boxford): Under 310 CMR 15.000 (Title 5), a state-certified Title 5 inspection ($500–$850) is legally mandatory within 2 years prior to any property transfer (extended to 3 years if you have receipts proving annual septic pumping).

💡 3 Ways Rockingham County Homeowners Save $100+ Per Pump-Out

  • 1. Expose Your Tank Covers Before the Truck Arrives (Saves $50–$125): Local haulers like C.B. LaFrance charge a minimum of $50 per foot/cover to dig up buried concrete lids. Uncovering the lid yourself or installing a surface grade riser saves on every future visit.
  • 2. Clean the Effluent Filter at Every Pump-Out (Included Free): Haulers like LaFrance, Bodwell's, and Kent Clean include effluent filter washing and baffle inspection at no extra charge during a routine pump-out — preventing slow drains and leach field backups.
  • 3. Book Before December Ground Freeze: Once New Hampshire soil freezes solid from December through March, digging buried lids or responding to an overdue tank backup can add $150–$300 in emergency winter fees.
